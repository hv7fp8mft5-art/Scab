Communications — PACE

Actions on

MAN OVERBOARD

Raise alarm; maintain visual; indicate direction; manoeuvre for recovery; slow/neutral as appropriate; recover casualty; headcount; assess; CASEVAC/report if required.

CAPSIZE

Account for all personnel; stay with craft unless directed otherwise; recover/assist as trained; secure equipment; re-establish communications; reassess.

BREAKDOWN

Stabilise craft and personnel; diagnose only when safe; establish PACE communications; arrange tow/recovery; conserve fuel/battery.

LOST / SEPARATED

Attempt PACE communications; establish known position; execute RV procedure; report if overdue; follow the agreed plan.

FOUL WEATHER

OIC directs; reduce exposure; seek safe shelter/landing; notify the group; recover craft when conditions exceed the plan.

CASUALTY / MASS CASUALTY

DRSABCD and first aid within training; establish casualty collection point if needed; request emergency services/CASEVAC; account for all personnel; record and report.

LOST COMMUNICATIONS

Attempt primary; move through PACE; move to agreed RV/known point; follow the prescribed lost-comms timing and report if overdue.

Data sources & limitations

Weather: Open-Meteo, including Australian ACCESS-G forecasts. Marine: Open-Meteo wave/swell/current model data. BOM is the Australian operational reference for marine warnings, coastal waters forecasts and tide predictions. Open-Meteo states its modelled sea-level/tide data has limited coastal accuracy and is not suitable for coastal navigation.
